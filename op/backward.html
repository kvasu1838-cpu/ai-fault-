CONCEPT

What is Backward Chaining?

Backward Chaining is a goal-driven inference technique. It starts with a possible conclusion and works backward to determine whether the required conditions are satisfied.

The system searches for rules that can prove the selected goal and then checks the conditions of those rules.

Goal

Example

Goal

Cooling System Failure

Rule

IF overheating AND fan is not working THEN cooling system failure

Required Facts

✔ Is the system overheating?

✔ Is the cooling fan working?

Conclusion

If both facts are confirmed, the goal is supported.

Forward vs Backward Chaining

Feature Forward Chaining Backward Chaining
Approach Data-driven Goal-driven
Starts with Facts Goal
Direction Facts → Conclusion Conclusion → Facts
